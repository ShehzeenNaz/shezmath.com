Methodology

How every calculator on this site works.

Standard formulas only

Each calculator uses one standard, published formula. Every tool page shows its formula and a worked example, so you can check the math yourself.

GPA scale

GPA tools use the common US 4.0 scale unless the page says otherwise: A = 4.0, B = 3.0, C = 2.0, D = 1.0, F = 0. Weighted GPA adds 1.0 point for AP courses and 0.5 for Honors, which is the most common US convention. Your school may use a different scale, so check your school's rules.

UK classifications

UK tools use the standard boundaries: First Class 70% and above, Upper Second (2:1) 60–69%, Lower Second (2:2) 50–59%, Third 40–49%. UCAS tariff points follow the published UCAS tariff tables.

Privacy by design

All calculations happen in your browser with JavaScript. Your grades never leave your device, and nothing you enter is stored or sent anywhere.

Corrections

If you find a wrong formula or a result that looks off, tell us at contact@shezmath.com and we will fix it.
