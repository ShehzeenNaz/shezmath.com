Privacy Policy

Your grades stay with you.

Calculations happen in your browser

Every calculator on ShezMath runs entirely in your web browser. The numbers you enter are never sent to our servers, stored, or shared. When you close the page, they are gone.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect names, email addresses, or grades. Basic anonymous visit counts may be collected by the hosting provider to keep the site running.

Cookies and ads

This site may show ads. Ad providers may use cookies to show relevant ads. You can turn cookies off in your browser settings.

Contact

If you email us, we use your email only to reply to you. We do not share it with anyone.

Questions about this policy: contact@shezmath.com.
