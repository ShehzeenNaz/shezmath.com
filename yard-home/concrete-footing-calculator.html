How It Works

Formula: Volume in cubic feet = length (ft) × width (in) ÷ 12 × depth (in) ÷ 12. Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27. Bags = cubic feet ÷ yield per bag.

Worked example: A footing 30 ft long, 12 in wide and 12 in deep: 30 × 1 × 1 = 30 cubic feet. 30 ÷ 27 = 1.11 cubic yards. With 80 lb bags: 30 ÷ 0.60 = 50 bags.

Frequently Asked Questions

How deep should a footing be?

Footings usually sit 12 inches deep and below the frost line in cold areas. Check local building rules for your area.

How wide should a footing be?

At least twice the width of the wall it carries, and never less than 12 inches wide for a house wall.

Can I pour footings with bagged concrete?

Yes for small runs. For long footings over a cubic yard, ready-mix is faster and more consistent.

How long before I can build on a footing?

Wait at least 7 days for the concrete to cure enough to carry a load, and 28 days for full strength.
