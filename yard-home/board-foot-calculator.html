How It Works

Formula: Board feet per board = thickness (in) × width (in) × length (ft) ÷ 12. Total = board feet per board × number of boards.

Worked example: A 2 in by 6 in board, 8 ft long: 2 × 6 × 8 ÷ 12 = 8 board feet per board. Ten boards: 8 × 10 = 80 board feet.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a board foot?

A board foot is a volume of wood 12 inches by 12 inches by 1 inch thick. It is how hardwood lumber is measured and sold.

How do I price lumber by board foot?

Multiply total board feet by the price per board foot. A $5 per board foot price on 80 board feet is $400.

Is a 2x4 really 2 inches by 4 inches?

No. A 2x4 is actually 1.5 inches by 3.5 inches after drying and planing. Use the real size in this calculator.

Do I need to add waste for board feet?

Yes, add 10% to 15% for cuts, defects and mistakes when ordering lumber for a project.
