How It Works

Formula: Rolls = wall area (sq ft) × 1.1 for waste ÷ coverage per roll (sq ft). A standard roll covers about 56 sq ft.

Worked example: A wall 12 ft by 8 ft with standard rolls: area is 96 sq ft. With 10% waste: 96 × 1.1 = 105.6 sq ft. 105.6 ÷ 56 = 1.89, so 2 rolls.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much wallpaper is in a roll?

A standard US roll is 20.5 inches wide by 33 feet long and covers about 56 square feet. European rolls are often smaller.

Why add 10% extra wallpaper?

Pattern matching wastes paper at every join. Large patterns can waste up to 20%, so add more for bold prints.

Do I need to match the pattern?

Yes, unless the paper is plain or random. Check the pattern repeat on the label before you buy.

Can I wallpaper over old wallpaper?

It is not ideal. Old paper can peel and show through. Strip it first for the best result.
