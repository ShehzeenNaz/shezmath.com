How It Works

Formula: Volume in cubic feet = length (ft) × width (ft) × thickness (in) ÷ 12. Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27. Bags = cubic feet ÷ yield per bag.

Worked example: A walkway 25 ft long, 3 ft wide and 4 in thick: 25 × 3 × 4 ÷ 12 = 25 cubic feet. 25 ÷ 27 = 0.93 cubic yards. With 80 lb bags: 25 ÷ 0.60 = 41.7, so 42 bags.

Frequently Asked Questions

How thick should a walkway be?

Four inches is standard for a walkway. Use five inches if it will carry heavy loads or sits on soft ground.

How wide should a garden walkway be?

Three feet is comfortable for one person and four feet lets two people walk side by side.

Should a walkway have control joints?

Yes, cut joints every 4 to 5 feet so cracks form in the joints instead of across the middle of the path.

Bags or ready-mix for a walkway?

Bags work for short paths under a cubic yard. Longer walkways are easier and cheaper with ready-mix.
