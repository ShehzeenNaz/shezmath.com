How It Works

Formula: Bags = lawn area (sq ft) ÷ coverage per bag (sq ft), rounded up. Total weight = bags × weight per bag.

Worked example: A lawn 40 ft by 25 ft with bags covering 5,000 sq ft: area is 1,000 sq ft. 1,000 ÷ 5,000 = 0.2, so 1 bag. With 20 lb bags, that is 20 lb of fertilizer.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know a bag's coverage?

It is printed on the bag label as square feet covered. Most lawn fertilizer bags cover 5,000 to 15,000 square feet.

How often should I fertilize my lawn?

Three to four times a year in spring, early summer, late summer and autumn. Follow the label for your grass type.

Can too much fertilizer burn the lawn?

Yes. Over-applying burns grass and leaves brown patches. Measure your lawn and stick to the bag rate.

Should I water after fertilizing?

Yes, water lightly after spreading granular fertilizer so it reaches the soil and starts working.
