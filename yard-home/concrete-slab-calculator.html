How It Works

Formula: Volume in cubic feet = length (ft) × width (ft) × thickness (in) ÷ 12. Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27. Bags = cubic feet ÷ yield per bag.

Worked example: A slab 12 ft by 10 ft at 4 in thick: 12 × 10 × 4 ÷ 12 = 40 cubic feet. 40 ÷ 27 = 1.48 cubic yards. With 80 lb bags at 0.60 cubic feet each: 40 ÷ 0.60 = 67 bags.

Frequently Asked Questions

How thick should a concrete slab be?

Four inches is standard for patios and shed bases. Use five to six inches for driveways and garage floors.

Should I order ready-mix or bags?

Bags suit jobs under about 1 cubic yard. Above that, a ready-mix truck is cheaper and gives a better finish.

How many 80 lb bags make a cubic yard?

Forty-five 80 lb bags make one cubic yard. Sixty 60 lb bags or ninety 40 lb bags do the same.

Do I need to add extra concrete?

Yes, add about 10% for spillage and uneven ground. Order a little more rather than running short mid-pour.
