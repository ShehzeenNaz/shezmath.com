Brick Calculator

Work out how many bricks a wall, planter or paver area needs. Includes a 5% waste allowance and the mortar bags to lay them.

How It Works

Formula: Bricks = wall area (sq ft) × bricks per square foot × 1.05 for waste. Mortar bags = bricks ÷ 40, because one 60 lb bag lays about 40 bricks.

Worked example: A wall 20 ft long and 4 ft high with standard bricks: area is 80 sq ft. 80 × 7 = 560 bricks. With 5% waste: 560 × 1.05 = 588 bricks. Mortar: 588 ÷ 40 = 14.7, so 15 bags.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many bricks are in a square foot?

About 7 standard bricks with mortar joints. Large bricks need about 4.5 and thin bricks about 10 per square foot.

How much mortar do I need?

One 60 lb bag of mortar lays about 40 standard bricks. Divide your brick count by 40 to get the bags.

Should I add waste bricks?

Yes, add 5% for cuts and breakage, and 10% if the wall has lots of corners or curves.

How many bricks are on a pallet?

A standard pallet holds about 500 bricks. Check with your supplier, as counts vary by brick size.
