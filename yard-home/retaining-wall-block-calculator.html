How It Works

Formula: Blocks per row = wall length in inches ÷ block length. Rows = wall height in inches ÷ block height. Total blocks = per row × rows × 1.05 for waste.

Worked example: A wall 20 ft long and 3 ft high with 16 in blocks: blocks per row are 240 ÷ 16 = 15. Rows are 36 ÷ 8 = 4.5, so 5 rows. Total is 15 × 5 = 75 blocks. With 5% waste: 75 × 1.05 = 78.75, so 79 blocks, plus 15 cap blocks.

Frequently Asked Questions

How high can I build a retaining wall?

Most areas allow up to 4 feet without an engineer. Taller walls need engineering and permits, so check local rules.

Do I need drainage behind a retaining wall?

Yes. Put 12 inches of gravel behind the wall and a drain pipe at the base, or water pressure will push the wall over.

Should the first row of blocks be buried?

Yes, bury the first row about 6 inches deep so the wall sits solid and does not slide forward.

What is the base for a retaining wall?

A 6 inch layer of compacted gravel under the first row. Never set blocks straight on soil.
