How It Works

Formula: Pavers = patio area (sq ft) ÷ area per paver (sq ft) × 1.05 for waste. Base gravel = area × 4 in depth ÷ 12 ÷ 27. Joint sand bags = area ÷ 80.

Worked example: A patio 15 ft by 12 ft with 12 by 12 in pavers: area is 180 sq ft. 180 ÷ 1 = 180 pavers. With 5% waste: 180 × 1.05 = 189 pavers. Base gravel: 180 × 4 ÷ 12 ÷ 27 = 2.22 cubic yards. Joint sand: 180 ÷ 80 = 2.25, so 3 bags.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many pavers do I need?

Divide the patio area by the area of one paver and add 5% waste. A 180 square foot patio needs 189 twelve-inch pavers.

How deep should the base be under a patio?

Four inches of compacted gravel for a patio, and six inches if cars will drive on it.

What size pavers are best for a patio?

Twelve by twelve inch pavers are the most popular. Larger pavers mean fewer joints and a faster lay.

Do I need edging for a paver patio?

Yes. Edge restraints stop the outer pavers spreading over time. Install them before you lay the pavers.
