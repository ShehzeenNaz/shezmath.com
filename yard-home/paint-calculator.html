How It Works

Formula: Gallons = wall area (sq ft) × coats ÷ coverage per gallon. One gallon of paint covers about 350 sq ft.

Worked example: A wall 12 ft by 8 ft with 2 coats: area is 96 sq ft. 96 × 2 = 192 sq ft to paint. 192 ÷ 350 = 0.55 gallons. Buy 1 gallon.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much paint do I need per room?

Measure each wall, add the areas and divide by 350 for one coat. Double it for two coats. A 12 by 12 ft room needs about 2 gallons for two coats.

Does primer count as a coat?

No. Primer is a separate coat that seals the surface. Count it apart from your two finish coats.

How much does a gallon of paint cover?

About 350 square feet on a smooth wall. Rough or porous walls use more, so coverage drops to about 250 square feet.

Should I buy extra paint?

Yes, buy one extra quart for touch-ups later. Paint colours vary slightly between batches.
