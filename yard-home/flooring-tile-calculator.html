Work out how many floor tiles a room needs, plus boxes. Enter the room size and tile size, and get counts with 10% waste.

How It Works

Formula: Tiles = room area (sq ft) ÷ area per tile (sq ft) × 1.1 for waste. Boxes = tiles ÷ tiles per box, rounded up.

Worked example: A room 12 ft by 10 ft with 12 by 12 in tiles: area is 120 sq ft. 120 ÷ 1 = 120 tiles. With 10% waste: 120 × 1.1 = 132 tiles. At 10 tiles per box: 132 ÷ 10 = 13.2, so 14 boxes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much extra tile should I buy?

Buy 10% extra for a straight layout and 15% for a diagonal layout or a room with many corners.

What size tile is best for a small bathroom?

Twelve by twelve inch tiles suit most bathrooms. Larger tiles make a small room look bigger but need more cuts.

Do I count the area under cabinets?

No, skip the area under fixed cabinets and baths. Measure only the floor you will see and walk on.

How many tiles are in a box?

It varies by tile size, often 8 to 12 tiles. Check the box label and enter that number in the calculator.
