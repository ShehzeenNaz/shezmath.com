How It Works

Formula: Cubic yards = length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (in) ÷ 12 ÷ 27. Tons = cubic yards × 1.3, because one cubic yard of sand weighs about 1.3 tons.

Worked example: A sandbox 10 ft by 8 ft at 6 in deep: 10 × 8 × 6 ÷ 12 = 40 cubic feet. 40 ÷ 27 = 1.48 cubic yards. 1.48 × 1.3 = 1.93 tons. With 50 lb bags: 40 ÷ 0.5 = 80 bags.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a cubic yard of sand weigh?

About 1.3 tons, or 2,600 lb. Wet sand weighs more, so order by volume.

What kind of sand do I need?

Play sand for sandboxes, coarse washed sand for paver bedding, and fill sand for levelling low spots.

How deep should sand be in a sandbox?

Six inches is enough for play. Deeper sand wastes money and makes the box hard to cover.

Bags or bulk sand?

Bags suit small jobs under half a cubic yard. For a sandbox or patio base, bulk delivery is far cheaper.
