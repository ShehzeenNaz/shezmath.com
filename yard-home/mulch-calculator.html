Work out mulch for flower beds and garden borders in cubic yards and bags. A 3 inch layer keeps weeds down and holds moisture in the soil.

How It Works

Formula: Cubic yards = length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (in) ÷ 12 ÷ 27. Bags = cubic yards × 27 ÷ cubic feet per bag.

Worked example: A bed 30 ft by 4 ft at 3 in deep: 30 × 4 × 3 ÷ 12 = 30 cubic feet. 30 ÷ 27 = 1.11 cubic yards. With 2 cubic foot bags: 30 ÷ 2 = 15 bags.

Frequently Asked Questions

How deep should mulch be?

Three inches is right for most beds. Less than two inches lets weeds through, and more than four inches can suffocate plant roots.

How many bags of mulch make a cubic yard?

A cubic yard is 27 cubic feet, so it takes about 14 two-cubic-foot bags or 9 three-cubic-foot bags.

Is bulk mulch cheaper than bags?

Yes, bulk mulch by the cubic yard is usually much cheaper than bags once you need more than about 2 cubic yards.

How often should I replace mulch?

Top it up once a year in spring. Mulch breaks down and thins out over the season.
