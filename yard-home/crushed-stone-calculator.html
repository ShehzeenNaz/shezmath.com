How It Works

Formula: Cubic yards = length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (in) ÷ 12 ÷ 27. Tons = cubic yards × the stone density in tons per cubic yard.

Worked example: An area 20 ft by 10 ft at 3 in deep with granite: 20 × 10 × 3 ÷ 12 = 50 cubic feet. 50 ÷ 27 = 1.85 cubic yards. 1.85 × 1.35 = 2.50 tons of granite.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a cubic yard of crushed stone weigh?

It depends on the stone. Granite is about 1.35 tons, limestone about 1.5 tons and pea gravel about 1.25 tons per cubic yard.

How deep should crushed stone be?

Use 2 to 3 inches for garden paths and 4 inches for driveways and areas with foot traffic.

Is crushed stone sold by the ton or the yard?

Both. Bulk suppliers often sell by the ton, while bagged stone is sold by weight. This calculator gives you both numbers.

Do I need landscape fabric under stone?

Yes for garden beds and paths. It stops weeds and keeps the stone from sinking into the soil.
