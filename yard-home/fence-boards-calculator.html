Work out how many fence boards or pickets a fence needs. Enter the run length, board width and any gap between boards.

How It Works

Formula: Boards = fence length in inches ÷ (board width + gap) × (1 + waste %). Rails = fence length ÷ 8 ft × 2 rails per section.

Worked example: A 100 ft fence with 6 in boards and no gap: 100 × 12 = 1,200 in. 1,200 ÷ 6 = 200 boards. With 5% waste: 200 × 1.05 = 210 boards. Rails: 100 ÷ 8 = 12.5 sections, so about 26 rails.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many fence boards do I need?

Divide the fence length in inches by the board width plus any gap. A 100 ft fence needs about 200 six-inch boards.

Should fence boards touch or have a gap?

Privacy fences use no gap. Picket fences use a gap of 1 to 3 inches between boards for a classic look.

How tall should a fence be?

Six feet is standard for privacy. Front yard fences are often 3 to 4 feet tall. Check local rules before building.

How many rails does a fence need?

Two rails per section for fences up to 5 feet tall, and three rails for 6 foot fences.
