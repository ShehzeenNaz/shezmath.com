How It Works

Formula: Length with waste = total length × (1 + waste %). Boards = length with waste ÷ board length, rounded up.

Worked example: You need 120 ft of lumber with 10 ft boards and 10% waste: 120 × 1.1 = 132 ft. 132 ÷ 10 = 13.2, so 14 boards.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much extra lumber should I buy?

Buy 10% extra for straight runs and 15% for jobs with many cuts, angles or short pieces.

What length boards should I buy?

Buy the longest boards your project allows. Fewer joints mean a stronger and neater result.

What is the difference between nominal and actual size?

A 2x4 is sold as 2 by 4 inches but actually measures 1.5 by 3.5 inches. Plan cuts with the real size.

Can I mix board lengths?

Yes, if your design allows joints in different places. It can cut waste on jobs with varied spans.
