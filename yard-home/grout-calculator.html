How It Works

Formula: Coverage per 25 lb bag = 150 sq ft × (tile size ÷ 12) × (0.125 ÷ joint width). Bags = area ÷ coverage, rounded up.

Worked example: A floor 12 ft by 10 ft with 12 in tiles and 1/8 in joints: area is 120 sq ft. Coverage is 150 × 1 × 1 = 150 sq ft per bag. 120 ÷ 150 = 0.8, so 1 bag of 25 lb grout.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much grout do I need?

One 25 lb bag covers about 150 square feet of 12 inch tile with 1/8 inch joints. Smaller tiles and wider joints use more grout.

Sanded or unsanded grout?

Use sanded grout for joints wider than 1/8 inch. Use unsanded grout for narrow joints and polished stone that scratches easily.

How much grout for a bathroom floor?

A typical 50 square foot bathroom floor with 12 inch tiles needs one 25 lb bag of grout.

Can I mix grout colours?

Do not mix different colours or brands. Buy all your grout from one batch so the colour matches.
