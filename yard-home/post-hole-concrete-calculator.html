How It Works

Formula: Volume per hole = π × (diameter ÷ 2)² × depth, all in feet. Bags per hole = volume per hole ÷ yield per bag, rounded up.

Worked example: One hole 12 in wide and 24 in deep: radius is 6 in, or 0.5 ft. 3.1416 × 0.5² × 2 = 1.57 cubic feet. With 80 lb bags at 0.60 cubic feet: 1.57 ÷ 0.60 = 2.62, so 3 bags per hole.

Frequently Asked Questions

How deep should a fence post hole be?

Dig one third of the post length, and at least 24 inches deep. In cold areas go below the frost line.

How many bags of concrete per post hole?

A standard 12 inch wide, 24 inch deep hole takes about 3 bags of 80 lb concrete. Wider or deeper holes take more.

Should concrete fill the whole hole?

Fill to about 3 inches below ground level and slope the top away from the post so water runs off.

Can I use dry concrete mix in a post hole?

Yes, pour dry mix around the post, add water and let it set. It works well for fence posts.
