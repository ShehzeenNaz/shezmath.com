Fence Post Calculator

Work out how many fence posts your fence needs and how far apart to set them. Includes concrete bags per post.

Your numbers never leave your device. All calculations happen in your browser.

How It Works

Formula: Sections = fence length ÷ post spacing, rounded up. Posts = sections + 1. Concrete bags = posts × bags per post.

Worked example: A 100 ft fence with 8 ft spacing: 100 ÷ 8 = 12.5, so 13 sections. Posts: 13 + 1 = 14 posts. With 1 bag per post, that is 14 bags of concrete.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far apart should fence posts be?

Six to eight feet apart is standard. Use six feet for tall or heavy fences and windy spots.

How many posts do I need?

Divide the fence length by the spacing and add one. A 100 ft fence at 8 ft spacing needs 14 posts.

How deep should fence posts go?

One third of the post length, and at least 24 inches deep. Deeper in soft soil or cold areas.

Do corners need extra posts?

Each corner and gate needs its own post. Add one post for every corner and two for a gate opening.
