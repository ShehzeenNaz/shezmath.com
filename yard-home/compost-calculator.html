How It Works

Formula: Cubic yards = length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (in) ÷ 12 ÷ 27. One cubic yard of compost weighs about 0.5 tons.

Worked example: A bed 20 ft by 8 ft at 2 in deep: 20 × 8 × 2 ÷ 12 = 26.67 cubic feet. 26.67 ÷ 27 = 0.99 cubic yards. With 1 cubic foot bags: 26.67 ÷ 1 = 27 bags.

Frequently Asked Questions

How deep should compost be?

Spread 2 to 3 inches over a bed and mix it into the top few inches of soil. One inch works as a yearly top dressing.

How much does a cubic yard of compost weigh?

About half a ton, or 1,000 lb. It is much lighter than topsoil or gravel.

Can I use too much compost?

Yes. More than 3 to 4 inches mixed in can hold too much water and unbalance nutrients. Stick to the depth above.

Compost or topsoil for a new bed?

Use topsoil to build up height and compost to feed the soil. Mix compost into the top layer of topsoil.
