BTU AC Size Calculator

Work out the BTU rating your room needs so the AC cools well without wasting power. Too small struggles, too big wastes money.

Your numbers never leave your device. All calculations happen in your browser.

How It Works

Formula: BTU = room area (sq ft) × 20 × (ceiling height ÷ 8) × sun factor. Add 600 BTU for each person above two. One ton of cooling = 12,000 BTU.

Worked example: A room 15 ft by 12 ft with 8 ft ceilings and normal sun: area is 180 sq ft. 180 × 20 = 3,600 BTU. With 2 people, no extra is added. That is 3,600 ÷ 12,000 = 0.3 tons, so buy a 6,000 BTU unit.

Frequently Asked Questions

What BTU do I need for my room?

Multiply the room area in square feet by 20. A 300 square foot room needs about 6,000 BTU.

What happens if my AC is too big?

It cools fast but shuts off before removing humidity, leaving the room cold and clammy. It also wastes power.

What happens if my AC is too small?

It runs all day, never reaches the set temperature and wears out faster. Size it right with this calculator.

Do sunny rooms need a bigger AC?

Yes, add 10% for very sunny rooms. Shaded rooms need 10% less. Kitchens add about 4,000 BTU for cooking heat.
