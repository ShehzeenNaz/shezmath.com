How It Works

Formula: Cubic yards = length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (in) ÷ 12 ÷ 27. One cubic yard of topsoil weighs about 1.1 tons.

Worked example: An area 20 ft by 15 ft at 6 in deep: 20 × 15 × 6 ÷ 12 = 150 cubic feet. 150 ÷ 27 = 5.56 cubic yards. With 0.75 cubic foot bags: 150 ÷ 0.75 = 200 bags.

Frequently Asked Questions

How deep should topsoil be for a lawn?

Four to six inches of topsoil over the subsoil gives grass roots room to grow. Less than that dries out fast.

How much does a cubic yard of topsoil weigh?

About 1.1 tons, or roughly 2,200 lb. Wet topsoil weighs more, so order by volume, not weight.

Bags or bulk topsoil?

Bags suit small patches. For anything over 2 cubic yards, bulk delivery is far cheaper.

Can I use topsoil to level a lawn?

Yes, spread it in thin layers of half an inch at a time and let the grass grow through between layers.
