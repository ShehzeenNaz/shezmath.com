How It Works

Formula: Bedding sand in cubic yards = area (sq ft) × bedding depth (in) ÷ 12 ÷ 27. Joint sand bags = area ÷ 80, because one 50 lb bag fills joints over about 80 sq ft.

Worked example: A patio 15 ft by 12 ft with 1 in of bedding sand: area is 180 sq ft. Bedding sand is 180 × 1 ÷ 12 ÷ 27 = 0.56 cubic yards. Joint sand is 180 ÷ 80 = 2.25, so 3 bags of 50 lb polymeric sand.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between bedding sand and joint sand?

Bedding sand is coarse sand under the pavers for levelling. Joint sand is fine polymeric sand swept into the gaps to lock pavers together.

How much joint sand do I need?

One 50 lb bag of polymeric sand covers about 80 square feet of standard pavers. Wider joints need more.

Can I use regular sand for paver joints?

It works for a while, but weeds grow through it and rain washes it out. Polymeric sand hardens and lasts far longer.

How deep should bedding sand be?

One inch, screeded level. More than that lets pavers shift and sink underfoot.
