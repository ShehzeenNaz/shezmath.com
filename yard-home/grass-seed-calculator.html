How It Works

Formula: Seed in pounds = lawn area (sq ft) ÷ 1,000 × seed rate in pounds per 1,000 sq ft. New lawns use 8 lb, overseeding uses 4 lb.

Worked example: A lawn 40 ft by 25 ft as a new lawn: area is 1,000 sq ft. 1,000 ÷ 1,000 × 8 = 8 lb of seed. That is 8 ÷ 5 = 1.6, so 2 bags of 5 lb.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much grass seed do I need?

A new lawn needs about 8 lb per 1,000 square feet. Overseeding an existing lawn needs about 4 lb per 1,000 square feet.

What happens if I sow too much seed?

Seedlings crowd each other, stay weak and die off. More seed does not make a thicker lawn.

When is the best time to sow grass seed?

Early autumn or spring, when the soil is warm and rain is regular. Avoid hot dry summer weeks.

How long until grass seed grows?

Most seed germinates in 7 to 14 days. Keep the soil damp every day until the new grass is about 3 inches tall.
