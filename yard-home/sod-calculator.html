Sod Calculator

Work out how much sod your lawn needs in square feet, rolls and pallets. Includes 5% extra for cuts and odd shapes.

Your numbers never leave your device. All calculations happen in your browser.

How It Works

Formula: Area = length (ft) × width (ft) × 1.05 for waste. Rolls = area ÷ 10, because one sod roll covers 10 sq ft. Pallets = area ÷ 500.

Worked example: A lawn 40 ft by 25 ft: area is 1,000 sq ft. With 5% waste: 1,000 × 1.05 = 1,050 sq ft. Rolls: 1,050 ÷ 10 = 105 rolls. Pallets: 1,050 ÷ 500 = 2.1, so 3 pallets.

Frequently Asked Questions

How big is a roll of sod?

A standard roll is 2 ft by 5 ft, covering 10 square feet. Some suppliers sell slabs of 16 by 24 inches instead.

How much sod is on a pallet?

A pallet usually covers about 500 square feet, or 50 rolls. Large pallets can cover 600 square feet.

How much waste should I allow for sod?

Allow 5% for a square lawn and 10% for a lawn with curves, trees or beds to cut around.

How soon can I walk on new sod?

Wait two to three weeks for the roots to take hold, and water it daily for the first two weeks.
