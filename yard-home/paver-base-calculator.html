How It Works

Formula: Base cubic yards = length (ft) × width (ft) × base depth (in) ÷ 12 ÷ 27. Tons = cubic yards × 1.4. Bedding sand uses a 1 inch layer.

Worked example: A patio 15 ft by 12 ft with a 4 in base: 15 × 12 × 4 ÷ 12 = 60 cubic feet. 60 ÷ 27 = 2.22 cubic yards. 2.22 × 1.4 = 3.11 tons. Bedding sand: 15 × 12 × 1 ÷ 12 ÷ 27 = 0.56 cubic yards.

Frequently Asked Questions

How deep should a paver base be?

Four inches for patios and paths, and six to eight inches for driveways that carry cars.

What gravel goes under pavers?

Crushed stone, often called road base or crusher run, compacts hard and drains well. Do not use pea gravel.

Do I need sand under pavers?

Yes, a one inch layer of coarse bedding sand sits on the compacted base and lets you level the pavers.

Should I compact the base?

Yes, compact it in layers of two inches at a time with a plate compactor, or the pavers will sink.
