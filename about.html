About ShezMath

Free calculators for one problem at a time: your grades.

ShezMath started with a simple idea. A student should not need one big confusing calculator for every grade question. Each question deserves its own small tool that answers it directly.

That is why this site has a separate calculator for each problem: what you need on a final, how many credits it takes to raise a GPA, what your UK degree class will be, how many classes you must attend to stay above a target, and more.

Every calculator on this site is free. Every calculation happens in your browser, so your grades never leave your device. See the Methodology page for how the math works.

Questions or a tool you would like added? Visit the Contact page.
