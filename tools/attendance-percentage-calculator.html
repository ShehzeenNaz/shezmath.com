How It Works

Formula: Attendance % = (Classes attended ÷ Total classes held) × 100. You can also find classes missed by subtracting attended classes from total classes held.

Worked example: You attended 42 classes out of 48 held. Divide 42 by 48 to get 0.875. Multiply by 100 to get 87.5%. Your attendance is 87.5%, and you missed 6 classes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is attendance percentage calculated?

Divide the number of classes you attended by the total number of classes held, then multiply by 100. For example, 42 attended out of 48 held is 42 divided by 48 times 100, which is 87.5%.

What is the minimum attendance required?

It depends on your school or university. Many require 75%, while some require 80% or 85%. Check your course handbook or ask your teacher for the exact rule.

What happens if my attendance is too low?

Low attendance can mean you are barred from exams, lose marks, or have to repeat a course. If you are below the limit, attend every class and speak to your teacher about your options.

Do late arrivals count as present?

That depends on your institution. Some mark a late arrival as present, others as absent or half present. Check your school's attendance policy to be sure.
