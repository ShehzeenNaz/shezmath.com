Two-Part Final Calculator

Some finals are split into two parts, such as a written paper and a practical. Enter each part's score and weight to find your final exam score and your overall course grade.

Your numbers never leave your device. All calculations happen in your browser.

How It Works

Formula: Final exam score = (part 1 score × part 1 weight) + (part 2 score × part 2 weight), with weights as shares of the final exam. Course grade = (current grade × (1 − final weight)) + (final exam score × final weight).

Worked example: Your current grade is 80% and the final is worth 40% of the course. Part 1 is 90% at 25% of the final and part 2 is 70% at 75% of the final. Final exam score = 90 × 0.25 + 70 × 0.75 = 22.5 + 52.5 = 75%. Course grade = 80 × 0.60 + 75 × 0.40 = 48 + 30 = 78%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if the two part weights do not add up to 100%?

The two part weights must add up to 100% of the final exam. If they do not, check your syllabus and fix the numbers, because the calculator cannot split the final correctly otherwise.

How do I know each part's weight?

Check your syllabus or ask your teacher. A common split is a written paper worth 60% of the final and a practical or oral part worth 40% of the final.

What is the final exam weight in the course?

It is the share of your whole course grade decided by the final exam. If the final is worth 40% of your course, enter 40. Your current grade covers the other 60%.

Can I use this for a final with more than two parts?

Not directly. Combine the smaller parts into one part first by working out their weighted average, then enter that combined score and its total weight as one part.
