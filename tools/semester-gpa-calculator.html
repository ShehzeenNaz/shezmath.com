Semester GPA Calculator

Work out your GPA for one semester on the standard 4.0 scale. Add up to four courses, pick each letter grade and enter the credit hours.

Your numbers never leave your device. All calculations happen in your browser.

How It Works

Formula: Semester GPA = total grade points ÷ total credits for the semester. Each course earns grade points equal to its grade value (A = 4, B = 3, C = 2, D = 1, F = 0) multiplied by its credit hours.

Worked example: Four courses this semester: B (3 credits), A (3 credits), B (4 credits) and A (3 credits). Grade points are 3 × 3 = 9, 4 × 3 = 12, 3 × 4 = 12 and 4 × 3 = 12. That is 9 + 12 + 12 + 12 = 45 grade points over 3 + 3 + 4 + 3 = 13 credits. 45 ÷ 13 = 3.46. Your semester GPA is 3.46.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between semester GPA and cumulative GPA?

Semester GPA covers one term only. Cumulative GPA covers all your terms so far. Your semester GPA feeds into your cumulative GPA, weighted by its credits.

What if I took more than four courses this semester?

Work out the GPA for four courses, then use the cumulative GPA calculator to combine that result with your remaining courses, treating each group as one block.

Do pass or fail courses count in my semester GPA?

Usually no. Pass or fail courses normally earn credit but no grade points, so leave them out of this calculator. Check your school policy if you are unsure.

How does my semester GPA affect my cumulative GPA?

A semester above your cumulative average pulls it up, and a semester below it pulls it down. The more credits you already have, the smaller the move. Use the cumulative GPA calculator to see it.
