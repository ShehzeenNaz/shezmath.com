UCAS Tariff Calculator

Add up your UCAS tariff points from up to three qualifications. Pick the qualification type and grade for each row to see your total points.

Your numbers never leave your device. All calculations happen in your browser.

How It Works

Formula: Total UCAS points = points for qualification 1 + points for qualification 2 + points for qualification 3. A-Level: A* = 56, A = 48, B = 40, C = 32, D = 24, E = 16. AS level: A = 20, B = 16, C = 12, D = 10, E = 6. BTEC: Distinction* = 56, Distinction = 48, Merit = 32, Pass = 16.

Worked example: You have an A-Level A (48 points), an A-Level B (40 points) and a BTEC Merit (32 points). Add them up: 48 + 40 + 32 = 120. Your total is 120 UCAS points.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many UCAS points is an A at A-Level worth?

An A at A-Level is worth 48 UCAS tariff points. An A star is worth 56, a B is 40, a C is 32, a D is 24 and an E is 16.

How many UCAS points is a BTEC Merit worth?

A BTEC Merit at National Extended Certificate level is worth 32 UCAS points. A Distinction star is 56, a Distinction is 48 and a Pass is 16.

How many UCAS points do I need for university?

It depends on the course. Many courses ask for 96 to 128 points, which is around three C to B grades at A-Level, while competitive courses can ask for 144 points or more. Check the entry requirements for each course.

Do AS levels count toward UCAS points?

Yes. An AS level grade A is worth 20 points, B is 16, C is 12, D is 10 and E is 6. AS levels are worth less than full A-Levels because they cover half the course.
