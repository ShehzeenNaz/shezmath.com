Grade Average Calculator

Find the average of up to five test scores in seconds. Enter your scores and see your average grade at a glance.

Your numbers never leave your device. All calculations happen in your browser.

How It Works

Formula: Average = (score 1 + score 2 + score 3 + score 4 + score 5) ÷ number of scores entered.

Worked example: Your five test scores are 78, 85, 92, 67 and 88. Add them up: 78 + 85 + 92 + 67 + 88 = 410. Divide by 5 tests: 410 ÷ 5 = 82. Your grade average is 82.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate the average of my test scores?

Add all your test scores together, then divide by the number of tests. For example, 78 plus 85 plus 92 plus 67 plus 88 is 410, and 410 divided by 5 is 82, so the average is 82.

What if I have fewer than five scores?

Leave the boxes you do not need empty. The calculator only averages the scores you enter, so two scores will be averaged over two, and three over three.

Is this a weighted average?

No. This is a simple average where every test counts the same. If your tests have different weights, use a weighted grade calculator instead.

Can I enter percentages and marks together?

Only if they are on the same scale. Convert marks to percentages first, or enter all scores as percentages, so the average is fair.
