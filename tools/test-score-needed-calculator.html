Test Score Needed Calculator

Want to lift your average with your next test? Enter your current average, how many tests you have taken, and your target average to find the exact score you need.

Your numbers never leave your device. All calculations happen in your browser.

How It Works

Formula: Needed score = (target average × (tests taken + 1)) − (current average × tests taken). The next test is counted as one test among the new total, so all tests count equally.

Worked example: Your average is 78% over 4 tests and you want an 80% average after 5 tests. Total marks so far = 78 × 4 = 312. Total marks needed after 5 tests = 80 × 5 = 400. Needed score = 400 − 312 = 88%. You need 88% on your next test.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if the score I need is over 100%?

If the needed score is above 100%, your target average cannot be reached with one more test. Aim for a lower target average, or plan to raise your average over several tests instead.

Does this assume all tests count equally?

Yes. This calculator treats every test as worth the same. If your tests have different weights, use the weighted grade calculator instead.

What should I enter for tests taken?

Enter how many tests you have already completed. The calculator adds one more test, so if you have taken 4 tests it works out the score needed on test 5.

Can I use this for quizzes or homework too?

Yes, as long as each quiz or assignment counts equally toward the average. Just enter your current average and how many you have done so far.
