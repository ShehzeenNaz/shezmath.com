Grade to Pass Calculator

Worried about passing? Enter your grade so far and how much work is left to find the lowest score you need on the remaining work to pass your course.

Your numbers never leave your device. All calculations happen in your browser.

How It Works

Formula: Needed grade = (pass mark − current grade × completed weight) ÷ remaining weight. Weights are used as decimals, so 60% completed is 0.60 and 40% remaining is 0.40.

Worked example: Your current grade is 55%, completed work is worth 60%, remaining work is worth 40%, and the pass mark is 50%. First, 55 × 0.60 = 33. Then 50 − 33 = 17. Finally, 17 ÷ 0.40 = 42.5%. You need at least 42.5% on the remaining work to pass.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if the grade I need is over 100%?

If the needed grade is above 100%, you cannot pass with the remaining work alone. Talk to your teacher about extra credit, a retake, or repeating the course.

What is the pass mark for my course?

Most courses set the pass mark at 50% or 60%, but it varies by school and subject. Check your syllabus or ask your teacher before entering a pass mark.

Do completed and remaining weights have to add up to 100%?

Yes. Completed work and remaining work together make up your whole course grade, so their weights must add up to 100%.

What does it mean if the needed grade is negative?

A negative result means you have already earned enough marks to pass, even if you score zero on the remaining work. You have passed the course already.
