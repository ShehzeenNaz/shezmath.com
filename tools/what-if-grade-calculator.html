What If Grade Calculator

See what your final grade will be if you score a certain mark on your remaining work. Perfect for planning how hard you need to work for your target grade.

Your numbers never leave your device. All calculations happen in your browser.

How It Works

Formula: Final grade = (current grade × completed weight ÷ 100) + (expected score × remaining weight ÷ 100).

Worked example: Your current grade is 82% and that work counts for 70% of the course. You expect 90% on the remaining work, which counts for 30%. First, 82 × 0.70 = 57.4. Then, 90 × 0.30 = 27. Add them together: 57.4 + 27 = 84.4. Your final grade would be 84.4%.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I work out my final grade if I know my score on the remaining work?

Multiply your current grade by the weight of the work you have finished, then add your expected score on the remaining work multiplied by its weight. For example, 82% on 70% of the course plus 90% on the remaining 30% gives 84.4%.

Do the completed and remaining weights have to add up to 100%?

Yes. The weight of the work you have finished and the weight of the work still to come must add up to 100% of the course, otherwise the result will not be a true final grade.

What if my expected score on the remaining work is only a guess?

Try a few values. Enter a low score, a realistic score and a high score to see the range of final grades you could end up with, then aim for the score that reaches your target grade.

Can I use this calculator for a single exam instead of all remaining work?

Yes. Treat the exam as the remaining work: enter your current grade, the weight of everything done so far, your expected exam score, and the exam's weight in the course.
