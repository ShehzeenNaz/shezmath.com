Drop Lowest Grade Calculator

Average your test scores after dropping the lowest one. Enter up to five scores and see your new average with the worst result removed.

Your numbers never leave your device. All calculations happen in your browser.

How It Works

Formula: Average after dropping the lowest = (sum of all scores − lowest score) ÷ (number of scores − 1). Only one score is dropped, even if two scores tie for the lowest.

Worked example: Your five test scores are 78, 85, 92, 67 and 88. The lowest is 67, so it is dropped. Add the rest: 78 + 85 + 92 + 88 = 343. Divide by the 4 remaining scores: 343 ÷ 4 = 85.75. Your average after dropping the lowest is 85.75. Without dropping it, your average would be 82.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do teachers drop the lowest grade?

To forgive one bad day. A single low score from illness or a rough week should not decide your whole grade, so many teachers drop the lowest test or homework score.

What if two of my scores tie for the lowest?

Only one of them is dropped. If two scores tie at 67, one 67 is removed and the other still counts in your average.

Can I drop more than one low score?

That depends on your teacher. Some drop the lowest two. This calculator drops one only. To drop two, run it once, then run it again on the remaining scores.

Does dropping the lowest grade always raise my average?

Yes, unless every score is the same. Removing a score below the average pulls the average up. The lower the dropped score, the bigger the rise.
