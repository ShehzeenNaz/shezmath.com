Credits to Raise GPA Calculator

Find out how many credits of A grades you need to reach your target GPA. Enter your current GPA, your credits so far and the GPA you want.

Your numbers never leave your device. All calculations happen in your browser.

How It Works

Formula: Solve (current GPA × credits + 4.0 × x) ÷ (credits + x) = target GPA for x, the credits of A (4.0) grades needed. That gives x = credits × (target − current GPA) ÷ (4.0 − target). The answer is rounded up to whole credits.

Worked example: Your GPA is 2.8 over 30 credits and your target is 3.0. Solve (2.8 × 30 + 4.0 × x) ÷ (30 + x) = 3.0. That is 84 + 4x = 90 + 3x, so x = 6. You need 6 credits of A grades, about two 3-credit courses, to reach a 3.0 GPA.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does this calculator assume I earn all As?

All As give the fastest possible climb, so the answer is the smallest number of credits that can reach your target. Earning Bs instead would take more credits.

What if I earn Bs instead of As?

Bs are worth 3.0 points, so they pull your average up more slowly. If your target is above 3.0, Bs alone can never reach it. Mix in As to close the gap faster.

Can I ever reach a 4.0 GPA?

Only if you have no credits below an A so far. Once a lower grade is on your record, even all As afterwards leave your average just under 4.0. The calculator tells you when a target is out of reach.

Would retaking a low grade help more than new credits?

Often yes. Retaking replaces the low grade instead of diluting it, so a few retaken credits can move your GPA more than many new ones. Try our retake GPA calculator to compare.
