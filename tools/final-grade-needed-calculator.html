Final Grade Needed Calculator

Work out exactly what score you need on your final exam to reach the course grade you want. Enter your current grade, how much the final is worth, and your target grade.

Your numbers never leave your device. All calculations happen in your browser.

How It Works

Formula: Needed final score = (desired grade − current grade × (1 − final weight)) ÷ final weight. Weights are used as decimals, so a 30% final is 0.30 and the rest of the course is 0.70.

Worked example: Your current grade is 85%, your final is worth 30%, and you want a 90% course grade. First, 85 × 0.70 = 59.5. Then 90 − 59.5 = 30.5. Finally, 30.5 ÷ 0.30 = 101.7%. You would need 101.7% on the final, which is impossible, so a 90% course grade cannot be reached.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if the score I need is over 100%?

If the calculator shows a needed score above 100%, your target grade cannot be reached, even with a perfect final exam. Lower your desired grade or check whether extra credit is available.

What does final exam weight mean?

The final exam weight is the share of your course grade decided by the final. If your final is worth 30% of your grade, enter 30. Your current grade covers the other 70%.

Can I use this calculator before all my coursework is graded?

Yes. Enter your best estimate of your current grade so far. The result is only as accurate as the grade you enter, so update it when new marks come in.

What score do I need to just pass the course?

Enter your pass mark, usually 50% or 60%, as your desired grade. The calculator will show the lowest final exam score that still lets you pass.
