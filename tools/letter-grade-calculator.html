How It Works

Formula: Match your percentage to the band it falls in: 93–100 A, 90–92 A−, 87–89 B+, 83–86 B, 80–82 B−, 77–79 C+, 73–76 C, 70–72 C−, 67–69 D+, 63–66 D, 60–62 D−, below 60 F.

Worked example: Your score is 88%. Look at the bands: 88 falls in the 87 to 89 range. That range is a B+. Your letter grade is B+.

Frequently Asked Questions

What letter grade is 88%?

88% is a B plus. On the standard scale, 87 to 89% is a B plus, 83 to 86% is a B, and 90 to 92% is an A minus.

What percentage do I need for an A?

On the standard scale used here, 93 to 100% is an A, and 90 to 92% is an A minus. Some schools use a different cut-off, such as 90% for an A, so check your own grading scale.

What is the lowest passing letter grade?

On this scale, a D minus at 60 to 62% is the lowest passing grade. Anything below 60% is an F, which means you did not pass.

Do all schools use the same letter grade bands?

No. The bands on this page are the common US standard, but schools and universities can set their own cut-offs, and some do not use plus and minus grades at all.
