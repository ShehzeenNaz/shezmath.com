GPA to Percentage Calculator

Turn your GPA on the 4.0 scale into a percentage. Useful for job forms, university applications and scholarships that ask for a percentage instead of a GPA.

Your numbers never leave your device. All calculations happen in your browser.

How It Works

Formula: Percentage = GPA × 25. This is the common linear conversion: a 4.0 GPA maps to 100% and a 0 GPA maps to 0%. It is a simple, widely used conversion, not an official standard, so check whether your university uses its own table.

Worked example: Your GPA is 3.5. Multiply by 25: 3.5 × 25 = 87.5. Your GPA converts to 87.5%.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you convert GPA to percentage?

With the common linear conversion, multiply your GPA by 25. A 4.0 GPA becomes 100%, a 3.0 GPA becomes 75%, and a 3.5 GPA becomes 87.5%.

Is GPA times 25 the official conversion?

No. It is the most common simple conversion, but there is no single official formula. Some universities use their own band tables, so always check what your institution accepts.

What percentage is a 3.0 GPA?

Using the linear conversion, 3.0 times 25 equals 75%. On a letter scale that is usually around a B grade.

Can I convert a GPA from a different scale?

This calculator is for the 4.0 scale only. If your GPA is on a 5.0 or 10.0 scale, convert it to the 4.0 scale first, or use percentage equals GPA divided by the maximum GPA, times 100.
