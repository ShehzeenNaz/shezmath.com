Final Grade Calculator

Find out what your final course grade will be before you sit the exam. Enter your current grade, the final exam weight and the score you expect.

Your numbers never leave your device. All calculations happen in your browser.

How It Works

Formula: Final grade = current grade × (1 − exam weight) + exam score × exam weight. The exam weight is written as a decimal, so a 30% exam counts as 0.30 and your current grade counts as 0.70.

Worked example: Your current grade is 85%, the final exam is worth 30% and you expect 90% on it. Your current grade counts for 70%: 85 × 0.70 = 59.5. The exam counts for 30%: 90 × 0.30 = 27. Add them: 59.5 + 27 = 86.5%. Your final grade is 86.5%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if my final exam is worth 40% of my grade?

Enter 40 as the final exam weight. Your current grade then counts for the other 60%. The calculator does the split for you, so any weight from 0 to 100 works.

Can I use this for a course graded out of points, not percent?

Yes. First turn your points into a percent: divide the points you earned by the points possible, then multiply by 100. Enter that percent as your current grade.

What score do I need on the final to pass the course?

Use our final grade needed calculator. It works backwards from the grade you want and tells you the exact final exam score you must earn.

Does the calculator round my final grade?

It shows your final grade to two decimal places. Most schools round to the nearest whole number, so 86.5% would usually count as 87%.
