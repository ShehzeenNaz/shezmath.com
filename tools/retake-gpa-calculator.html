Retake GPA Calculator

See how retaking a course changes your GPA. Enter your current GPA and credits, then the old and new grades for the course you retake.

Your numbers never leave your device. All calculations happen in your browser.

How It Works

Formula: New GPA = (current GPA × total credits − old grade points × retaken credits + new grade points × retaken credits) ÷ total credits. The old grade is taken out and the new grade is put in its place.

Worked example: Your GPA is 3.0 over 30 credits and you retake a 3-credit course, moving from a C (2.0) to an A (4.0). Your grade points now: 3.0 × 30 = 90. Take out the old C: 90 − (2.0 × 3) = 84. Add the new A: 84 + (4.0 × 3) = 96. 96 ÷ 30 = 3.20. Your new GPA is 3.20.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does my school replace the old grade or average the two?

Most schools replace the old grade with the new one in your GPA, though the old grade stays on your transcript. Some schools average both attempts instead. Check your school policy before you rely on this result.

What if I retake a course worth different credits?

Enter the credits of the course you are retaking. If the new attempt is worth different credits than the old one, use the credits of the new attempt and check with your school how it counts.

Can retaking a course lower my GPA?

Yes, if your new grade is lower than the old one. Retaking only helps when the new grade beats the grade it replaces. This calculator shows the change either way.

What grade points is each letter grade worth?

On the 4.0 scale, A is worth 4.0, B is worth 3.0, C is worth 2.0, D is worth 1.0 and F is worth 0. Enter those numbers as the old and new grade points.
