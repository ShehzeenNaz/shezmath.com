Test Score Percentage Calculator

Turn your marks into a percentage in seconds. Enter the marks you scored and the total marks for the test, and see your result as a percentage.

Your numbers never leave your device. All calculations happen in your browser.

How It Works

Formula: Percentage = (marks obtained ÷ total marks) × 100.

Worked example: You scored 42 marks out of a total of 50. Divide 42 by 50 to get 0.84. Multiply 0.84 by 100 to get 84. Your test score is 84%.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my test score as a percentage?

Divide the marks you obtained by the total marks, then multiply by 100. For example, 42 marks out of 50 is 42 divided by 50, which is 0.84, times 100 gives 84%.

What is the difference between marks and percentage?

Marks are the raw score you earned, such as 42 out of 50. A percentage turns that score into a value out of 100, so 42 out of 50 becomes 84%, which makes it easy to compare tests with different totals.

Can I use this calculator for any total marks?

Yes. Enter any total, whether the test was out of 20, 50, 100 or any other number, and the calculator will convert your marks into a percentage.

What percentage is a pass?

It depends on your school or university. Many use 40% or 50% as the pass mark, while some courses need 60% or more. Check your course handbook for the exact pass mark.
