Weighted Grade Calculator

Not every assignment counts the same. Enter up to four scores with their weights to work out your weighted course grade in seconds.

Your numbers never leave your device. All calculations happen in your browser.

How It Works

Formula: Weighted grade = (score 1 × weight 1 + score 2 × weight 2 + score 3 × weight 3 + score 4 × weight 4) ÷ total weight. Leave a row at 0% weight to ignore it.

Worked example: Your scores are 90% at 20%, 80% at 30%, 95% at 10% and 85% at 40%. Multiply each score by its weight: 90 × 20 = 1800, 80 × 30 = 2400, 95 × 10 = 950, 85 × 40 = 3400. Add them up: 1800 + 2400 + 950 + 3400 = 8550. Divide by the total weight, 100: 8550 ÷ 100 = 85.5%. Your weighted grade is 85.5%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a weighted grade?

A weighted grade gives more importance to some assignments than others. A final exam worth 40% counts four times as much as homework worth 10%, so each score is multiplied by its weight before averaging.

Do the weights have to add up to 100%?

For a final course grade they should add up to 100%. If your weights add up to less, the calculator still works by dividing by the total weight you entered, which is useful for a part-finished course.

What if I have more than four assignments?

Combine similar assignments into one row first. For example, average all your homework scores into one homework score, then enter that score with the total homework weight.

What is the difference between a weighted and a simple average?

A simple average treats every score equally. A weighted average counts each score according to its weight, so a big exam moves your grade far more than a small quiz.
