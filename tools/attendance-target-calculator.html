Attendance Target Calculator

Find out how many classes in a row you must attend to reach your target attendance. Enter your numbers and get the exact count in seconds.

Your numbers never leave your device. All calculations happen in your browser.

How It Works

Formula: If you attend x more classes in a row, your new attendance is (attended + x) ÷ (total + x). Set that equal to your target and solve for x: x = (target × total − attended) ÷ (1 − target), using the target as a decimal, then round up to the next whole class. If your current attendance already meets the target, x is 0. A target of 100% can never be reached once a class has been missed.

Worked example: You attended 30 out of 45 classes and your target is 75%. Your current attendance is 30 ÷ 45 = 66.7%. Solve (30 + x) ÷ (45 + x) = 0.75. That gives 30 + x = 33.75 + 0.75x, so 0.25x = 3.75, and x = 15. You need to attend 15 classes in a row. Check: 45 attended out of 60 held is exactly 75%.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many classes in a row do I need to attend to reach 75%?

It depends on your current attendance. Enter your classes attended, total classes held and your target of 75% in the calculator, and it will tell you the exact number of classes in a row you must attend.

What if my target is already met?

If your current attendance is already at or above your target, you need 0 more classes in a row. You can still miss a few classes before you fall below the target again.

Can I still reach 100% attendance?

No. Once you have missed a class, you can never reach exactly 100% again, because the missed class stays in your total. You can get very close by attending every class from now on.

What if I miss one more class?

Missing a class lowers your attendance and increases the number of classes in a row you must attend afterwards. Re-run the calculator with your new numbers to see the updated count.
