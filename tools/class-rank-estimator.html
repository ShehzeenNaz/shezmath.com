Class Rank Estimator

Curious where you stand in your class? Enter your GPA and class size to estimate your rank and percentile. This is a statistical estimate, not an official rank.

Your numbers never leave your device. All calculations happen in your browser.

How It Works

Formula: Assume class GPAs are normally distributed with mean 3.0 and standard deviation 0.4. Z-score = (your GPA − mean) ÷ standard deviation. Your percentile is the normal CDF of the z-score. Estimated rank = class size × (1 − percentile).

Worked example: Your GPA is 3.6 in a class of 200. Z-score = (3.6 − 3.0) ÷ 0.4 = 1.5. A z-score of 1.5 is about the 93.3rd percentile, so you are above roughly 93.3% of your class. Rank = 200 × (1 − 0.933) = 200 × 0.067 = 13.4, so your estimated rank is about 13th out of 200. Remember, this is an estimate, not an official rank.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this my official class rank?

No. This is only an estimate based on a normal distribution model. Your school's official rank comes from the real GPAs of every student in your class, which this calculator cannot see.

Why assume GPAs follow a normal distribution?

In a large class, GPAs often spread out in a rough bell shape around the average. It is a reasonable model for an estimate, but real classes can be skewed, so the result is approximate.

What are the mean and standard deviation for?

The mean is the average GPA in your class, assumed to be 3.0, and the standard deviation, assumed to be 0.4, is how spread out GPAs are. Change them if you know your class is stronger or more spread out.

What does a percentile of 93% mean?

It means your GPA is higher than about 93% of your classmates. In a class of 200, that puts you around rank 13, since about 7% of 200 students, or 14 students, are ahead of you.
