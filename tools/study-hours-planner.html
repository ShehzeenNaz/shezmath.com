Study Hours Planner

Split your study hours across subjects before exam day. Tell us your days left, daily hours and each subject's importance to get a fair plan.

Your numbers never leave your device. All calculations happen in your browser.

How It Works

Formula: Total hours = Days left × Hours per day. Each subject gets: Total hours × (Subject importance ÷ Sum of all importance scores). A subject with a higher importance score gets a bigger share of your time.

Worked example: You have 7 days left and 3 hours a day, so total hours = 7 × 3 = 21 hours. Your subjects have importance scores of 5, 3 and 2, which add up to 10. Subject 1 gets 21 × (5 ÷ 10) = 10.5 hours. Subject 2 gets 21 × (3 ÷ 10) = 6.3 hours. Subject 3 gets 21 × (2 ÷ 10) = 4.2 hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

How should I split study time between subjects?

Split your total hours in proportion to each subject's importance. A subject with a higher weight, more marks or an earlier exam gets more hours. This planner does that split for you automatically.

What does the importance number mean?

Importance is a score from 1 to 5. Give 5 to your most important subject, such as one worth the most marks or the one you find hardest, and 1 to the least important.

How many hours a day should I study?

Most students do well with 2 to 4 focused hours a day, with short breaks every 25 to 50 minutes. Long sessions without breaks lead to poor focus and weak memory.

What if I have more than 3 subjects?

Plan your 3 most important subjects here first, then run the planner again for the rest. Or group smaller subjects together and treat the group as one subject.
