UK Year Weighting Calculator

Work out your final UK degree mark when each year counts for a different amount. Enter your year averages and their weights to see your mark and degree class.

How It Works

Formula: Final mark = (Year 2 average × Year 2 weight + Year 3 average × Year 3 weight) ÷ 100. The weights must add up to 100. Degree class: 70% or more is a First, 60–69% is a 2:1, 50–59% is a 2:2, and 40–49% is a Third.

Worked example: Year 2 average 65% at 40% weight, and year 3 average 72% at 60% weight. Multiply each mark by its weight: 65 × 40 = 2600, and 72 × 60 = 4320. Add them: 2600 + 4320 = 6920. Divide by 100: 6920 ÷ 100 = 69.2%. The final mark is 69.2%, which is a 2:1.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are year weightings in a UK degree?

Year weightings say how much each year of study counts toward your final degree mark. A common split is 40% for year 2 and 60% for year 3, while year 1 often counts for 0%.

Do the weights have to add up to 100?

Yes. The weights of the years that count must add up to 100%. If they do not, the calculator warns you and the result will be wrong, so check your course handbook for the correct split.

What mark do I need for a First?

You need a final weighted mark of 70% or more for a First. A mark of 60 to 69 is a 2:1, 50 to 59 is a 2:2, and 40 to 49 is a Third.

Does year 1 count toward my degree?

At most UK universities year 1 does not count toward your final mark. You still need to pass it to progress, but it is usually weighted at 0%.
